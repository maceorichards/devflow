# Third-party notices

devflow builds its managed skills from two MIT-licensed upstreams, cloned into `vendor/` at the commits in `vendor.lock`:

- [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), by Jesse Vincent.
- [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), by Matt Pocock.

What in this repo derives from them:

- `patches/`: diffs against upstream skills from both repos, carrying their text.
- `owned/tdd`: forked once from Matt Pocock's `tdd` skill (see `owned/tdd/ORIGIN.md`).

The upstream skills themselves aren't in this repo: each user's sync builds them from clones it pulls itself.
